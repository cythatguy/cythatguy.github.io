The Show

Sleeping Jesus is a five piece outfit based in Minneapolis, MN. With 3 full length albums, a few EPS, and a handful of singles under their belt. They’ve traveled the United States playing their shimmery brand of Americana, Indie, and Rock and Roll for the last 10 years. They are just as excited to start the journey for the next 10. They only hope you’ll be there singing along on the journey.

The new record from Sleeping Jesus, "Shotgun", is full of rock and roll and whimsy. Like the scatter of shotgun, on their third and fully self produced record, Sleeping Jesus scatters themselves across multiple genres, from steel guitar filled shit-kickin' country, 70s reminiscent folk, and bouncy harmony filled indie rock. Pressed on 180g vinyl and shipped to your front door.
