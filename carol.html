The Play

Two Dickensian orphans try to present their own version of A Christmas Carol. They’ve only got each other and a handful of props, but their fingers are going numb, so they might need some help. What could go wrong? Come for a fresh, madcap take on a heartwarming classic, stay for the never-ending thrill of audience participation!
